DAWNKEY PCB fabrication outputs

Board: nominal 94 x 90 mm, two copper layers.
Copper: DawnKey-F_Cu.gtl (front), DawnKey-B_Cu.gbl (back).
Mask: DawnKey-F_Mask.gts (front), DawnKey-B_Mask.gbs (back).
Silkscreen: DawnKey-F_Silkscreen.gto (front), DawnKey-B_Silkscreen.gbo (back).
Outline: DawnKey-Edge_Cuts.gm1.
Drills: DawnKey-PTH.drl (plated) and DawnKey-NPTH.drl (non-plated), Excellon, millimeters, absolute origin.

Review every layer and drill file in the board house's Gerber viewer before ordering. Material, copper weight, finish and tolerances are not prescribed here. See the project-level Production/Preflight.md: the design remains a prototype pending full KiCad DRC and resolution of a KiCad/FreeRouting round-trip discrepancy.
